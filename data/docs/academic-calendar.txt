UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC CALENDAR — ODD SEMESTER 2026-27
====================================================

JULY 2026
- Jul 20 (Mon): Semester begins. Regular classes commence for all B.Tech year 2 and year 3 students.
- Jul 27 (Mon): Last date for registration without late fee. Late fee is Rs. 500 per week thereafter.

AUGUST 2026
- Aug 10 (Mon): Add/drop window closes at 4:00 PM. Courses cannot be changed after this date.
- Aug 15 (Sat): Independence Day — holiday.
- Aug 28 (Fri): Onam celebration — holiday for all departments.

SEPTEMBER 2026
- Sep 07 (Mon): First internal assessment test (IA-1) begins for all regulated courses.
- Sep 11 (Fri): IA-1 ends. IA-1 carries 10 marks in the 50-mark internal total.
- Sep 24 (Thu): Mid-semester break begins.

OCTOBER 2026
- Oct 01 (Thu): Mid-semester break ends. Classes resume Oct 2 (Fri).
- Oct 12 (Mon): Second internal assessment test (IA-2) window opens.
- Oct 16 (Fri): IA-2 ends. IA-2 carries 10 marks.
- Oct 20 (Tue): Assignment and mini-project submissions due for all S5 courses.

NOVEMBER 2026
- Nov 02 (Mon): Preparation holiday begins.
- Nov 09 (Mon): End-semester examinations begin for all semesters.
- Nov 25 (Wed): End-semester examinations end.

DECEMBER 2026
- Dec 08 (Tue): Results expected on the university portal.
- Dec 15 (Mon): Even semester registration begins.

INTERNAL MARKS STRUCTURE
The 50-mark internal component splits as: IA-1 = 10, IA-2 = 10, assignments and lab = 10,
attendance = 5, and a capstone mini-project = 15. Attendance below 75 percent attracts a
one-grade deduction unless a medical certificate is filed within 7 working days.
