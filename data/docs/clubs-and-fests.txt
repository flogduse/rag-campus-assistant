STUDENT CLUBS AND ANNUAL TECH FEST
===================================

TECH FEST — "PRAVYOM 2027"
The annual national-level tech fest runs for three days in the third week of January
(estimated Jan 15-17, 2027). Events include a 24-hour hackathon with a Rs. 50,000 prize
pool, robotics arena, line-follower race, UI/UX sprint, and a startup pitch with incubation
offers. Registration opens in December through the student portal; team size is 2-4 members.

MAJOR CLUBS
Robotics Club — meets Wednesdays 4:30 PM, ECE block lab 2. Builds line followers, drones
and sumo bots. Faculty advisor: Dr. Menon.

Coding Club — meets Fridays 5:00 PM, CS block seminar hall. Weekly contests, ICPC
preparation, and a peer-mentored DSA track for first-years.

AI/ML Society — meets Saturdays 10:00 AM. Current projects: a campus RAG assistant,
a sign-language translator, and a gesture-control interface. Open to all branches;
basic Python required.

Electronics Hobby Club — open lab every Saturday 2:00 PM. Soldering stations and
oscilloscopes available after a safety induction.

STARTUP AND E-CELL
The Entrepreneurship Cell runs a monthly "Idea Friday" and connects student teams to
the incubation center. Seed grants of up to Rs. 25,000 are available twice a year.

HOW TO JOIN
Club recruitment drives happen in the second week of each odd semester. Sign-ups are
on the student activities portal; most clubs take a short interaction rather than a test.
